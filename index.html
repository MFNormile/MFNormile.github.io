Cool Facts About Cephalopods

Cephalopods are some of the most bizzare animals on our planet. They include octopuses, squids, and cuttlefish. They are exceptionally intelligent and have been observed to not only solve puzzels but cephalopods in captivity can become lathargic if not mentally stimulated. A lot of cephalopods can also change color to hide from predators or communicate with each other. Some cephalopods have the ability to shoot ink which can be used as a distraction to escape predators. You can learn more about them on Wikipedia or check out the Britannica article.
